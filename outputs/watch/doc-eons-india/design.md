Here is an art direction and motion-graphics breakdown of **PBS Eons: *When India Was An Island***.

---

### 1. ON-SCREEN TEXT LAYOUT

#### A. Subject / Taxon Labels (Lower-Third Badges)
* **Position:** Bottom-left corner (approx. 10% from bottom, 5% from left margin).
* **Size:** ~5–7% of total frame height.
* **Font Style:** Bold, clean sans-serif (caps/title case).
* **Colour:** Dark charcoal/black text on a bright pastel yellow (#FFE566 guess) rounded rectangle ("pill") badge.
* **Background:** Solid yellow fill with rounded corners, no drop shadow.
* **Animation:** Smooth 0.3s horizontal slide-in from the left edge paired with a slight fade-in; holds for ~3–5 seconds, then slides back left or fades out.
* **Examples:** 
  * `01:58` – `Allopatric Speciation`
  * `02:19` – `Jainosaurus`
  * `02:24` – `Indosuchus`
  * `02:26` – `Rajasaurus`
  * `08:02` – `Cambaytherium`
  * `08:40` – `Indohyus`
  * `09:42` – `Dipterocarps`

#### B. Source & Image Credit Callouts
* **Position:** Bottom-right corner.
* **Size:** ~3–4% of frame height.
* **Font Style:** Regular sans-serif, small, clean.
* **Colour:** Dark charcoal text inside a solid white rectangular box with a thin dark border/shadow.
* **Animation:** Instant cut or quick 0.2s fade-in whenever new artwork, maps, or paper figures are shown.
* **Examples:**
  * `00:12` – `Credit: C.R. Scotese`
  * `01:28` – `Credit: Hannes Grobe`
  * `01:36` – `Credit: Brownfield, M.E., and Schenk, C.J. (USGS)`
  * `02:06` – `Credit: Merdith et al. 2021`
  * `08:02` – `Credit: Elaine Kasmer`

#### C. Section Header / Category Titles
* **Position:** Dead-center of frame over dark background.
* **Size:** ~12–15% frame height.
* **Font Style:** Heavy, extra-bold sans-serif (`Ungulates` at `07:40`).
* **Colour:** Solid pure white, no outline.
* **Animation:** Fade-in from 0% to 100% over 0.4s.

#### D. Avoiding Clutter & Stacking
* **Layout Rule:** Screen real estate is split logically—**Lower Left** = subject name/concept badge, **Lower Right** = image credits, **Top Right / Side** = secondary floating inset card (`03:38`, `05:00`, `06:24`, `07:02`). Text rarely exceeds 2–3 words per badge, ensuring zero overlap with host or main artwork.

### 2. TIME / ERA INDICATORS

#### A. Map Header Timestamps
* **Position:** Top-center or top-left above paleogeographic maps.
* **Design:** Clean white sans-serif text (`129 Ma`, `98 Ma`, `82 Ma`, `67 Ma`).
* **Animation:** Flashes/cuts to new million-year mark synchronously as the map morphs to a new geological epoch (`02:06`, `02:34`, `03:14`, `03:56`, `05:34`).

#### B. Full-Screen Horizontal Timeline Bar
* **Position:** Bottom-third of frame (`06:18`, `06:46`, `09:02`).
* **Height:** ~15% frame height.
* **Design:** 
  * Continuous multi-colored horizontal bar representing geological eras:
    * **Purple / Teal / Green:** Mesozoic Era segments.
    * **Orange / Coral:** Cenozoic Era segment.
    * **Yellow:** Present day mark.
  * **Milestone Labels:** Thin vertical white tick-marks with fine white leader lines extending upward/downward. Text is white sans-serif detailing key events (e.g., `120-100 MYA - India and Madagascar break away from Africa`, `67 MYA - Deccan Traps`, `66 MYA - K-Pg Impact`, `49 MYA - Protocetids evolve`).
* **Animation:** The timeline bar glides upward into frame. Ticks and event text pop on with a subtle opacity fade synced precisely with the narrator’s timeline references.

### 3. MAPS

* **Style & Render:** Mix of full 3D globes (`00:04`, `01:06`, `08:48`), flat paleogeographic reconstructions showing ocean depth gradients (deep blue to turquoise shelf waters), and topographical land shading.
* **Colour Palette:** Deep ocean navy (`#0B1B3D`), coastal azure (`#2089B2`), land tan/khaki (`#9E8B5E`), and forest greens.
* **Tectonic & Directional Motion:**
  * **Plate Boundaries:** Bold dark-red line vectors showing active fault lines (`02:06`, `03:14`).
  * **Plate Velocity / Drift Vectors:** Animated bright yellow/orange arrows indicating direction and speed of continental migration (`03:24`, `07:12`).
  * **Boundary Highlights:** Red vector line drawing across map terrain to highlight newly formed geographic barriers (e.g., the Himalayan crest at `10:46`).
* **Map Labels:** Floating white text badges (e.g., `TETHYS SEA` at `03:34`) or white rectangular callout boxes with pointer arrows (e.g., `Africa`, `Madagascar`, `India` at `01:28`).
* **Camera Moves:** Continuous slow linear zoom-in or panning across ocean basins to maintain visual interest over static 5-second voiceover segments.

* **Show Bumper Card (`01:18`, `10:56`):**
  * **Visual:** Full-frame deep-sea blue particle background with floating marine life silhouettes.

### 6. THE 10 MOST REUSABLE DESIGN RULES

1. **Yellow-Pill Lower Thirds for Taxa/Concepts:** Use a consistent bright yellow rounded badge with black bold text for key scientific names to instantly grab eyes without cluttering artwork.
2. **Standardized Credit Boxes:** Always place third-party image/map credits in the extreme bottom-right inside a small white box to maintain professional academic attribution.
3. **Color-Coded Horizontal Timeline Bar:** Anchor long historical or geological sequences with a color-segmented timeline bar across the lower-third, using fine white leader lines for milestone dates.
4. **Dedicated Anchor Locations:** Reserve specific screen zones for specific UI elements (Bottom-Left = Subject; Bottom-Right = Credit; Top-Right = Floating Inset Graphic).
5. **Vector Motion Arrows on Paleomaps:** Animate directional arrows (yellow/orange) over map graphics to explain complex continental movements clearly.
6. **Red Accent Vector Tracing:** Use thin, high-contrast red lines to trace landmass breakups, fault lines, or mountain range boundaries on geographical maps.
7. **Framed Picture-in-Picture Insets:** When host is on screen, show supporting graphics inside rectangular floating cards with thin white borders and soft drop shadows instead of full-screen cuts.
8. **Consistent Map Palette:** Maintain unified ocean blues, continental tans, and shelf turquoise across different map sources for visual continuity.
9. **Minimalist Top Mark Epoch Headers:** Show geological dates (`129 Ma`, `67 Ma`) at top-center/top-left in plain white sans-serif to keep viewer context without obstructing map visuals.
10. **High-Contrast Area Highlights:** Highlight land/volume stats by filling the affected region with a single flat accent color (e.g., solid red for volcanic basalt flow over a grey/yellow base map) paired with a simple square legend key.

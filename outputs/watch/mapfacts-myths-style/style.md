### 2. Pacing

*   **Average Shot Length:** ~4.5 seconds per shot.
*   **Longest Hold:** ~9 seconds (01:46 - 01:55, river rapids B-roll establishing gravity concept).
*   **Cuts Per Myth:** 3 to 4 cuts per myth segment (Satellite Intro -> Real B-roll -> Map Scale Reveal/Outro).
*   **Myth Card / Title Duration:** Myth transitions do not use full-screen static title cards; instead, they rely on rapid satellite map zooms (0.5s–1.0s) coupled with voiceover numbering ("Number 8", "Number 7") to maintain momentum.

---

### 3. Text and Captions

*   **Caption Style:** Single-to-two-word kinetic captions centered near lower third, yellow/white text with dark stroke/shadow.
*   **Title / Location Cards:** High-contrast yellow text inside dark rectangular badges (e.g., `MOUNT EVEREST`, `Red Sea`).
*   **Stat Callouts:** Large bold white sans-serif overlays for scale multipliers (e.g., `5X`, `4X`).
*   **Graphics & Emojis:** Minimal emojis; uses geometric map pins, solid vector country fills, and a large red `X` graphic for debunking claims.

---

### 4. Map and Reveal Techniques

*   **Solid Vector Fills:** Bright primary colors (Red, Green, Blue, Cyan) applied as opacity masks over landmasses to highlight specific political borders or bodies of water.
*   **Comparative Scaling:** Quick camera pans between distant continents (Alaska $\rightarrow$ Brazil $\rightarrow$ Australia) accompanied by relative size multipliers (`5X`, `4X`).
*   **Photorealistic Context B-Roll:** Intercutting flat satellite top-down views with eye-level ground footage (e.g., scuba diving for sub-surface elevation, rocky desert cliffs for non-sandy terrain).
*   **Negative Feedback Visuals:** Red `X` graphics stamp over incorrect geographic assumptions before transitioning to the true location.

---

### 5. Sound Design

*   **Music:** Upbeat, steady electronic/synth rhythm track maintaining high drive without dynamic dips.
*   **Sound Effects (SFX):**
    *   Low sub-bass whooshes on rapid map zooms.
    *   Crisp UI clicks/pops when location pins drop or country fills illuminate.
    *   Negative "thud" or subtle riser when the Red `X` appears.
*   **Silence:** No absolute silence; audio bed remains continuous across all segment transitions.

---

### 6. Retention Devices

*   **Hook (00:00–00:10):** Begins with a challenge question ("Do you think geography is boring?") followed by a numerical countdown structure ("8 biggest geography lies").
*   **Pacing Rhythm:** Alternating between high-level satellite maps and immersive real-world video clips every 3–4 seconds prevents visual fatigue.
*   **Cognitive Dissonance:** Debunking widely taught facts creates curiosity loops (e.g., showing a sea that isn't red, a mountain taller than Everest).
*   **Retention Weaknesses:**
    *   Visual mislabeling (e.g., text label displays "MOUNT AYA" while referencing Mauna Kea/Hawaii, accompanied by a picture of Mount Fuji).
    *   Repetitive satellite zoom transitions toward the second half.

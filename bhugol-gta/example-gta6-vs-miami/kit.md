## Description
GTA 6 launches November 19th — so we put the trailers next to the real map of Miami and Florida. Ten stops: a hotel copied twice, sinking towers, an apartment that's gone, the murals, the arena, the airport sign, the Everglades, a sugar town, the Keys… and mountains Florida never had. Plus the moment Miami started copying the game back.

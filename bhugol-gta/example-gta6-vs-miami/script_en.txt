STOP 1 · OCEAN DRIVE
10 Stop one. *Ocean Drive*, Miami Beach.
11 Pastel Art Deco hotels, neon signs, palm trees, and *the beach* right across the road.
12 In the trailers, it's called *Ocean Beach*. Same strip. Same pastel *colours*.
13 The Colony Hotel becomes the *Cortez*. Hotel Victor becomes Hotel *Dixon*.
14 And here's the thing. Rockstar has copied this street *before*.
15 In *2002's* Vice City, that same Colony Hotel was the *Colon* Hotel.
16 Same building. Copied twice. *Twenty-four years apart.*
17 That hotel is still standing. But a few blocks away, two towers are slowly going *down*.
STOP 2 · THE TOWERS
18 Stop two. The *towers*.
19 Vice City's skyline copies real Miami Beach condos, like the *Floridian* and *Murano Grande*.
20 In 2024, University of Miami scientists measured buildings along this coast, from Miami Beach to Sunny Isles.
21 They found *35* towers that had sunk. Two to eight centimetres, in just *seven years*.
22 Local reporters found both of those copied towers *on the list*.
23 In the game, they stand perfectly still. In real life, they're *sinking*.
24 Some Miami buildings are sinking. One is already *gone*.
STOP 3 · THE APARTMENT
25 Stop three. The *apartment*.
26 GTA 6's two leads, Jason and Lucia, share a small pink apartment by the bay.
27 Fans matched it to a real building: the *Belmar*, a 1973 condo in Miami's *Edgewater*.
28 But in *2022*, a developer bought every unit for about *12 million dollars*... and tore it down.
29 Today, that spot is an *empty lot*.
30 So the only place you can still visit the Belmar... is *Vice City*.
31 And a few streets west, Rockstar copied Miami's *walls*.
STOP 4 · THE MURALS
32 Stop four. *Wynwood*.
33 Miami's mural district. Old warehouses, painted from the pavement to the *roof*.
34 In the game, it's called the *Stockyards*. Same warehouses. Same walls of *colour*.
35 From the murals, it's a short drive downtown. To the *arena*.
STOP 5 · THE ARENA
36 Stop five. Downtown's *basketball arena*.
37 The real one is the *Kaseya Center*, home of the Miami *Heat*.
38 In the game, it's the *Sahara Arena*, home of the Vice City *Narcos*.
39 Then, in 2026, a helicopter lowered giant pink letters onto the *real* arena's roof.
40 They read: *"Welcome to Vice City."*
41 The county's price for that sign? *975,000 dollars.*
42 Just *25,000* below the amount that would have needed a public vote.
43 But the game's most famous sign was copied from somewhere even *stranger*.
STOP 6 · THE AIRPORT
44 Stop six. *Miami International Airport*.
45 In the trailer, giant white letters spelling *"VICE"* stand at the end of the runway.
46 The real airport has *no sign* like that.
47 But for the movie *Bad Boys*, Hollywood built one there anyway. Giant letters spelling *"MIAMI"*.
48 A movie prop... that became a *video game landmark*.
49 Now leave the city. Drive west. And the copying gets *wilder*.
STOP 7 · THE SWAMP
50 Stop seven. The *Everglades*.
51 In the game, they're called the *Grassrivers*.
52 That name comes from a *1947* book that called the Everglades a *"River of Grass."*
53 The book helped save the swamp. The same year, the Everglades became a *national park*.
54 The game's swamp has alligators, flamingos and airboats. Just like the real one.
55 It even hides *Burmese pythons* in the grass.
56 In the real Everglades, pythons are an *invasive species*. Pet snakes that escaped, and took over.
57 Head north from the swamp, and you hit something much *sweeter*.
STOP 8 · THE SUGAR TOWN
58 Stop eight. *Clewiston*, on Lake Okeechobee.
59 Florida's biggest lake. Surrounded by *sugar cane*.
60 Clewiston calls itself *"America's Sweetest Town."*
61 Fans matched it to the game's *Ambrosia*. Its slogan: *"Keeping Leonida Sweet."*
62 The real sugar company becomes *Allied Crystal*. The lake becomes *Lake Leonida*.
63 Rockstar copied the town... and the *sales pitch*.
64 Now turn south. All the way south. To the end of the *road*.
STOP 9 · THE KEYS
65 Stop nine. The *Florida Keys*.
66 A chain of islands, stitched together by bridges, like the *Seven Mile Bridge*.
67 In the game: the *Leonida Keys*. Same island chain. Same *long bridge*.
68 In the real Keys, a roadside village has a *giant lobster*. Nine metres tall. Her name is *Betsy*.
69 In the game, Betsy is gone. In her place: a giant *whale*.
70 Florida has islands, beaches, swamps and sugar. What it does *not* have... is mountains.
STOP 10 · THE MOUNTAINS
71 Stop ten. *Mount Kalaga.*
72 Florida's highest natural point is *Britton Hill*. Just *105 metres*.
73 That's the *lowest* top point of any US state.
74 Plenty of Miami *skyscrapers* are taller than Florida's tallest hill.
75 But GTA 6's Florida has a whole mountain *national park*.
76 Fans traced its canyons to *Providence Canyon*, in neighbouring *Georgia*.
77 So the copy has something the original *doesn't*.
78 And that's when the original started copying the *copy*.

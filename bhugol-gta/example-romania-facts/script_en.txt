FACT 8 · LITTLE PARIS
9 Number eight. Romania's capital, *Bucharest*, once wanted to be *Paris*.
10 Around a hundred years ago, people called it *Little Paris*. Grand boulevards, French-style palaces, cafés everywhere.
11 And then Bucharest went one step further. It built its own *Arc de Triomphe*.
12 The first one was made of *wood*, in 1878, just so the army could march under it after independence.
13 Today's stone arch was finished in *1936*, and it's twenty-seven metres tall. Paris has the original. Bucharest has the *copy*.
14 But copying Paris is normal. Number seven is a cemetery that tells *jokes*.
FACT 7 · THE MERRY CEMETERY
15 Number seven. The *Merry Cemetery*, in the village of Săpânța.
16 In 1935, a local artist named Stan Ioan Pătraș carved a wooden cross for a grave and painted it *bright blue*.
17 Then he added a painting of the person's *life*. And a short poem about them.
18 And the poems are *funny*. Some of them roast the person who died. Some of them roast their *mother-in-law*.
19 Today there are more than *eight hundred* of these crosses. A cemetery where people go to *laugh*.
20 Which is a pretty Romanian way to deal with death. And speaking of the undead...
FACT 6 · NOT DRACULA'S CASTLE
21 Number six. *Bran Castle*, in Transylvania. Sold all over the world as *Dracula's Castle*.
22 Here's the problem. Bram Stoker, who wrote Dracula, never *visited* Romania.
23 There's no evidence he ever saw this castle. It isn't even *in* the book.
24 And the real Vlad the Impaler, the prince behind the name? Most historians say he never *lived* here.
25 So the most famous vampire castle on Earth has almost nothing to do with the *vampire*.
26 Today it belongs to an *Austrian archduke*, and it's Romania's first private museum. Dracula's castle has a *landlord*.
27 Now, from a fake vampire to a real dictator. Number five is a *road*.
FACT 5 · THE ROAD BUILT ON FEAR
28 Number five. The *Transfăgărășan*.
29 In 1968, the Soviet Union invaded Czechoslovakia. And Romania's leader, Nicolae *Ceaușescu*, got nervous.
30 What if the Soviets came for Romania next? His army needed a fast way across the *Carpathian Mountains*.
31 So from 1970, they blasted a road straight over them. Hairpin after hairpin, up to *2,042 metres*.
32 The invasion never came. But the road is still there. And in 2009, Top Gear called it *the best road in the world*.
33 One catch. Snow closes it for most of the year, usually from late October to late *June*.
34 And those mountains have residents who don't care about any of this. Big ones. With *claws*.
FACT 4 · BEAR COUNTRY
35 Number four. Romania has more *brown bears* than any country in Europe outside Russia.
36 Scientists counted them with *DNA*, from more than twenty-four thousand samples of hair and droppings.
37 Their estimate: between *ten thousand six hundred* and almost *thirteen thousand* bears.
38 In Brașov County alone, there are around *thirteen hundred*.
39 And they don't always stay in the forest. Bears wander into towns, through streets, and straight into people's *bins*.
40 So yes, in Romania, you might need bear spray to take out the *trash*.
41 But number three is a building so big, the bears could get *lost* in it.
FACT 3 · THE HEAVIEST BUILDING
42 Number three. The *Palace of the Parliament*, in Bucharest.
43 Guinness World Records lists it as the *heaviest building in the world*. About *four million tonnes*.
44 It has around *eleven hundred* rooms. In 2013, only about four hundred were actually in *use*.
45 Ceaușescu ordered it in *1984*. To make room, about seven square kilometres of old Bucharest were knocked down.
46 *Forty thousand* people had to move out.
47 And it cost so much to heat and light that the bills run to over *six million dollars* a year.
48 And the dictator who ordered it never saw it finished. He was overthrown in *1989*.
49 Number two goes the other way. Not up. *Down*.
FACT 2 · THE UNDERGROUND THEME PARK
50 Number two. *Salina Turda*, a salt mine in Transylvania.
51 People have been digging salt here since at least *1271*.
52 By hand, the miners carved out chambers almost a *hundred metres* tall.
53 The mine closed in 1932. And then Romania did something wild with it.
54 It turned the bottom of the mine into a *theme park*. Deep underground.
55 There's a *Ferris wheel*. Bowling. Mini golf. Table tennis. An amphitheatre.
56 And in the deepest chamber, an underground lake, where you can rent a *rowing boat*.
57 It's the only place I know where you can go bowling inside a *salt shaker*.
58 And number one is the place on this list you can *never* visit.
FACT 1 · THE SEALED CAVE
59 Number one. *Movile Cave*, near the Black Sea.
60 In 1986, workers were digging near the town of Mangalia, and they broke into a cave nobody knew *existed*.
61 Scientists found that it had been cut off from the outside world for about *five and a half million years*.
62 No sunlight. Almost no fresh air. The air inside has only about *seven to ten percent* oxygen. Ours has twenty-one.
63 And yet it's full of *life*.
64 Fifty-seven animal species. And *thirty-seven* of them exist nowhere else on Earth.
65 Spiders, leeches, water scorpions, all living in total darkness on bacteria that eat *chemicals* instead of sunlight.
66 It's a tiny world that evolved completely on its *own*.
67 And to protect it, only a few scientists a year are allowed *inside*.
68 So Romania has the heaviest building on Earth, and a cave almost nobody will ever *see*.

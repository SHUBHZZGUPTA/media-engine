# ep02 art plan: Black Wednesday

**Style sentence (same as ep01, appended to every painted prompt):**
> Cinematic painterly illustration for a dark, mysterious history documentary: rich oil-painting texture, chiaroscuro lighting with warm amber-gold highlights against deep navy-blue shadows, muted desaturated palette, light haze, shallow depth of field, composed like a film still with the main subject slightly off-centre and calm darker space in the lower third for captions. No text, no letters, no numbers, no writing, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no borders. No recognizable real people; any people are anonymous, seen from behind, in silhouette or partly in shadow. Landscape 1536x1024, opaque PNG.

**Hard rules for this episode:**
- No living public figures are drawn: Soros, Druckenmiller, Lamont, Major and Schlesinger never appear as faces. They are shown through places, rooms and silhouettes from behind.
- No real banknote designs and no readable screens or newspapers. Any money or paper is generic and unreadable.
- Times, rates and numbers are engine overlays (slams and counts), never painted in.

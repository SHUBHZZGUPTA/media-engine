# SHORT: DRIP PRICING
N: That *$100* ticket becomes *$130* at checkout. That's no accident.
N: It's called *drip pricing:* the fees drip in at the very *end.*
N: In a *StubHub* experiment with millions of users, hiding fees until checkout made people spend about *21%* more.
N: And they were about *14%* more likely to buy at all.
N: Why? By checkout, you've picked your seats. They already feel like *yours.*
N: So in *May 2025,* a US rule made ticket sellers and hotels show the *full price* up front.
N: Taxes can still come later. But mandatory fees? No more *surprises.*
N: That's the mirage: the price you see first isn't the price. It's the *bait.*
N: So judge every deal by the *total* at checkout, not the number that *hooked* you.

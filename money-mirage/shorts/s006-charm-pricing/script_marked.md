# SHORT: WHY PRICES END IN .99
N: Why does everything cost *$9.99?* Because your brain reads it as *$9.*
N: It's called the *left-digit effect.* You read a price left to right, and the first digit *sticks.*
N: *$10* feels like ten. *$9.99* feels like nine-something. One cent moves a whole *dollar* in your head.
N: It even works on cars. Across *22 million* auction sales, used cars sold for about *$200* less once the odometer crossed a *10,000*-mile mark.
N: But here's the weird part. In one experiment, a dress sold *better* at *$39* than at *$34.*
N: Cheaper, and fewer buyers. Why? Years of sales taught us that a *9* means *bargain.*
N: That's the mirage: the 9 isn't a discount. It's a *signal* that feels like one.
N: So round up. *$9.99* is *ten dollars.*

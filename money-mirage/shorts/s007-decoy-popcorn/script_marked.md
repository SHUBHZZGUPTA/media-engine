# SHORT: THE DECOY THAT MIGHT BE A MIRAGE
N: Why does the medium popcorn cost almost as much as the large? It might be a *decoy.*
N: Picture it: small, *$3.* Large, *$7.* Now add a medium at *$6.50.*
N: Suddenly the large looks like a *steal.* The medium isn't there to sell. It's there to make the large look *cheap.*
N: The famous proof? An Economist offer: web, *$59.* Print, *$125.* Print plus web, also *$125.*
N: Dan Ariely said *84%* of his students picked the combo. Remove the useless print option, and only *32%* did.
N: But here's the twist. That test used *students,* not real subscribers.
N: *Two* of Ariely's other papers have since been *retracted* over data problems. And bigger studies found the decoy effect is often *weak* in real life.
N: That's the mirage: even the trick about tricks can be an *illusion.*
N: So don't fear the medium. Just ask: would I buy the large *without* it?

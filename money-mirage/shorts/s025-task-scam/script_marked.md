# SHORT: PAID TO LIKE VIDEOS
N: A stranger texts you: get paid just for *liking videos.* And the first payment is *real.*
N: The US regulator, the *FTC,* calls this a *task scam.*
N: You like videos or rate products, in sets of *forty,* and an app shows your *earnings* climbing.
N: You withdraw a few dollars. It *works.*
GOLDIE: [smug, deep, rich] See? Easy money. Now, just a tiny deposit.
N: Then a *"double task"* appears. To unlock it, you must *deposit* your own money, usually in *crypto.*
N: Each level asks for *more.* The balance on screen keeps growing, but it was never *real.*
N: Reports jumped from about *5,000* in all of 2023 to about *20,000* in just the first half of *2024.*
N: The Better Business Bureau says the typical task-scam victim lost about *$2,300* in 2025.
N: That's the *mirage:* the first few dollars are *bait* for your deposit.
N: The FTC's rule is simple: *never pay to get paid.*

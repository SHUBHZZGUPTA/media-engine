# SHORT: THE BLACK FRIDAY DEAL THAT ISN'T
N: Your Black Friday deal could be cheaper in *January.* One TV was cheaper on *134 days* after the sale. So is the deal *real?*
N: Consumer group *Which?* tracked *175* products at eight big retailers for a whole *year.*
N: *83%* were the same price or cheaper at some other time.
GOLDIE: [smug, deep, rich] Shocking. Truly. I'm shocked.
N: So how does an ordinary price look like a *deal?*
N: The *"was"* price. Discounts are often measured from a price the item rarely sold at.
N: Add a *countdown* clock, and you stop checking.
N: And the trick doesn't stop on Friday.
N: Prices often *drop again* after the sale. That TV later sold for *27%* less.
N: That's the *mirage:* the discount is measured from a price nobody *paid.*
N: The real test is the *price history,* not the *percent off.*

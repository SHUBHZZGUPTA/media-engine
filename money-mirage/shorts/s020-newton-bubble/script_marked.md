# SHORT: THE GENIUS WHO LOST TO A BUBBLE
N: The man who discovered *gravity* lost up to *£20,000* in a *bubble.* Could it happen to *you?*
N: In *1720,* all of London wanted shares in the *South Sea Company.*
N: In six months, the price jumped from about *£130* to over *£1,000.*
GOLDIE: [smug, deep, rich] Even I don't rise that fast.
N: So what did the smartest man in England *do?*
N: He *sold* most of his shares early. A smart, tidy *profit.*
N: Then prices kept *climbing.* Everyone around him kept getting *richer.*
N: And that's where genius stopped helping.
N: He *bought back in,* at roughly *double* the price. Then the bubble *burst.*
N: Isaac *Newton* lost a fortune. Historians still debate exactly *how much.*
N: That's the *mirage:* being *smart* doesn't protect you from the *crowd.*
N: He supposedly said he could calculate the *stars,* but not the *madness* of people.

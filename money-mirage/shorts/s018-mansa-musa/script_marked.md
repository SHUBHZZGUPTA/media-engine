# SHORT: THE KING WHO CRASHED GOLD
N: One king's trip to *Cairo* made gold *cheaper* for *12 years.* How do you make gold lose *value?*
N: In *1324,* *Mansa Musa,* ruler of *Mali,* stopped there on his way to *Mecca.*
N: The story goes his caravan carried *tons* of gold.
GOLDIE: [smug, deep, rich] Finally, a man who understood me.
N: So how did one man *do* that?
N: He *gave* it away. To officials, to emirs, to the *treasury.* His people *spent* it everywhere.
N: Suddenly, Cairo had *more gold* than it knew what to do with.
N: And then things got really strange.
N: A gold coin worth *25* silver coins fell to *22,* or less.
N: A Cairo writer said it stayed low for about *12 years.*
N: That's the *mirage:* gold isn't valuable for being *gold.* It's valuable for being *rare.*
BILL: [delighted, squeaky] So I'm rare too?
N: Flood a city with it, and even a *king's* gold becomes *ordinary.*
N: Comment: what would you do with a ton of gold?
N: This is *Money Mirage.* *Follow* for more.

# SHORT: THE COIN THAT COST TOO MUCH
N: Every penny in your change cost America almost *4 cents* to make. So why keep making it?
N: In *2024,* the Mint spent about *3.7 cents* on each one-cent coin. A *loss* on every single one.
GOLDIE: [smug, deep, rich] Losing money to make money. Bold.
N: So why didn't they stop *sooner?*
N: Pennies go back to *1793.* Habit, tradition, and partly a fear that shops would round prices *up.*
N: Then the Treasury placed its *final* order for penny blanks.
N: The last one was struck in *Philadelphia* in November *2025.*
N: And here's the coin that's even worse.
N: The *nickel* also costs more than *5 cents* to make. And it's still being made.
N: That's the *mirage:* a coin's value is *printed* on it, not *built* into it.
N: Over *114 billion* pennies are still out there, still *legal tender.*
BILL: [delighted, squeaky] Finally, a coin with a worse job than me!
N: Comment: do you still use pennies?
N: This is *Money Mirage.* *Follow* for more.

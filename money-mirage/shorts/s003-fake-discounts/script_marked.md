# SHORT: THE "WAS" PRICE THAT NEVER WAS
N: That *70% off* tag? The price it's off from may *never have existed.*
N: Here's the trick. Right before a sale, the *"was" price* goes up.
N: *₹1,999,* crossed out. Now *₹999.* You feel like you *won.*
N: But say it sold for *₹999* all last month anyway.
N: Your brain anchors on the *first number* it sees. That's the whole game.
N: So why don't shops get caught? Nobody had to show the *real* old price.
N: That's changing. From *January 2027,* India's new rule puts the *lowest price* of the last *30 days* next to every discount.
N: Europe already has this rule. In an EU check of *314* online shops, about *30%* still showed discounts wrong.
N: That's the mirage: the discount isn't counted from the *real* price. It's counted from a *made-up* one.
N: So ignore the crossed-out number. Check the *price history.*
N: Sometimes the best deal was *always* this price.

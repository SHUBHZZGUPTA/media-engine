# SHORT: TULIP MANIA, 1637
N: In *1637,* legend says a single tulip bulb cost as much as a *house* in Amsterdam.
N: The star was the *Semper Augustus:* white petals with flames of red.
N: Its price climbed from about *1,200* guilders to a reported *10,000.*
N: People traded bulbs still in the *ground,* on paper contracts in *taverns.*
N: Then, in *February 1637,* at an auction in Haarlem, nobody bid. Within a week, prices *collapsed.*
N: The story says the Dutch economy was *ruined.* Families lost *everything.*
N: But here's the twist. Historian Anne Goldgar searched the records and couldn't find anyone who went *bankrupt.*
N: Much of the drama comes from a book written *200* years later.
N: That's the mirage: history's most famous bubble was partly a *bubble* of a story.
N: So when you hear everyone lost everything, check who's *telling* it.

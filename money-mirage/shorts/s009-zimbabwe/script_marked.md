# SHORT: THE $100 TRILLION NOTE
N: This note says *100 trillion dollars.* On its first day, it was worth about *$30.*
N: How do you get there? In *November 2008,* prices in Zimbabwe were rising about *98%* a day.
N: They doubled roughly every *25 hours.* Monthly inflation hit an estimated *79.6 billion* percent.
N: The government kept cutting zeros off the money. *25* of them, in three rounds.
N: Then, in *April 2009,* it gave up. People switched to *US dollars* and rand.
N: In *2015,* the old money was finally swapped: *$1* for *35 quadrillion* Zimbabwe dollars.
N: Your *100 trillion* note? About *40 cents.*
N: Here's the twist: collectors now pay around *$100* for one.
N: That's the mirage: a number on paper isn't wealth. Money is only worth what people *trust.*

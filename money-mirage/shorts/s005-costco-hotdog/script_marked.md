# SHORT: THE $1.50 HOT DOG
N: Costco sells you a hot dog and a drink for *$1.50.* It's cost that since *1985.*
N: Adjust for inflation, and it'd be around *$4.50* today. So why hasn't it moved?
N: The story goes, the founder told his successor: raise the hot dog, and I'll *kill* you.
N: Because the hot dog isn't the product. *You* are.
N: It's a *loss leader:* a deal so cheap it pulls you into the store.
N: Last year, Costco sold over *245 million* of those combos.
N: But to get in, you need a *membership.*
N: In its latest year, Costco made about *$5.9 billion* from fees alone.
N: That's roughly *two-thirds* of its whole profit.
N: That's the mirage: the cheap hot dog isn't losing money. It's selling the *membership.*
N: So when a deal looks too good, ask what it's *really* selling you.

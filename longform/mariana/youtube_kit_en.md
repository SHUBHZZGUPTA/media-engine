## Description
In 2019, a man reached the deepest point on Earth, almost 11 km down... and found a plastic bag waiting for him. But that's not the strangest thing in the Mariana Trench. We dive all the way down: where light dies, where the pressure would crush a submarine, where fish glow and shrimp grow giant, and where the planet is slowly swallowing the ocean.

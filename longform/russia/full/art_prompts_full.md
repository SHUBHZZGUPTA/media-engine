# Russia full video — new art for chapters 2–11 (Codex prompts, for approval)

Same style and template as the pilot art (`longform/russia/art/gen.sh`): toy-figure characters on transparent PNGs, painterly sets with empty ground for the characters. 22 characters + 16 sets = 38 images. Script: `longform/russia/art2/gen.sh`.
Real historical people and photos (Ivan III, Godunov, Peter's portrait, Nicholas II's family, Stalin, Gagarin, Gorbachev, Putin, etc.) come from public-domain / CC archive images, not new art. Putin appears only in archive photos and places, never as a character.

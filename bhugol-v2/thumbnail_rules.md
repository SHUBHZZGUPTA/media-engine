# BHUGOL thumbnail rules (from the 20 Oct 2026 thumbnail study)

Evidence: 903 Shorts from 10 geography channels (blind visual labels on each channel's 12 best and 12 worst
Shorts by views vs the channel median, plus automatic colour/text measurements on all 903), and a literature review
(Eden 507k-video study, vidIQ breakout study, 1of10, MrBeast team, YouTube Shorts docs).

## Must
1. **No characters, people or faces.** Faces: in 14% of the top group vs 21% of the bottom group (top led in only 1 of 5 channels). Cartoon characters: 3% vs 8%.
2. **The map is the hero.** Satellite or illustrated map, never a photo scene (photo scenes: 12% of the top group vs 16% of the bottom).
3. **Colour in the subject region**, ideally in **its flag's colours** (flag-filled country/region). Highlighted region: top +6 pts; flag: +3 pts, and the highest views/subscribers of any feature.
4. **A red hand-drawn circle/outline + arrow** on the exact spot the video pays off. Strongest feature: 23% in the top group vs 13% in the bottom, top led in 5 of 6 channels.
5. **2–3 "mind-blowing" emoji stickers** (🤯 😱 + the country flag emoji). Emoji/icons: +3 pts, top led in 6 of 9 channels.
6. **1–3 words of headline text**, never 7+ (7+ words: median 0.89x the channel's median views vs 1.06x for 1–3 words). The text must not repeat the title (yt-package rule).
7. **High saturation, with red accents.** These were the only pixel measures that leaned positive across channels (saturation 5 of 6, red 6 of 7). Brightness and contrast alone didn't matter.
8. **A number when the title doesn't already use it** (+3 pts).
9. **Readable at feed size.** Before shipping, shrink it to about 140 px wide; the region, circle and words must survive (yt-audit rule).
10. Badge small, top-left. Keep key content centred: the 9:16 frame is cropped on some surfaces.

## Make two versions
- Vertical 1080x1920: also burned into frames 0–2 of the MP4 (the cover-picker trick).
- 16:9 1280x720: rotate or re-crop so the coloured regions still read (e.g. a flag laid horizontally); text left, subject right.

## Why it matters less than the hook
- About 99.9% of Shorts views come from the Shorts feed, where no thumbnail is shown (YouTube, Jan 2025).
- Thumbnails matter on the Home page, search, the channel page and Subscriptions. So the first frame and hook come first; the thumbnail is the second lever.
- All effects here are correlations, not proof.

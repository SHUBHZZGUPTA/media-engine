# BHUGOL voice.md (v1, 20 Oct 2026)

Built from 7 published BHUGOL scripts: Point Nemo, Great Wall of Trees, Time Zones, Koreas, Stone Money,
Everest, India's Hidden Superpower. Every YT skill and the BHUGOL v2 skill read this file before writing.

## Who we're talking to
Curious viewers across the world scrolling Shorts (English video, plus a Hindi version), who love
"wait, really?" geography facts. They're smart and busy, and they don't want a lecture.

## How BHUGOL sounds
- Short, punchy sentences (5-15 words). Fragments are fine: "No river. No mountains. Just a number."
- Line 1 is an "impossible" present-tense claim, often with a number: "There's a place on Earth where the
  closest humans are in space." Line 2 twists it further: "And something has been falling into it since 1971."
- Re-ask the question mid-story: "So how did...?", "So why has nobody ended it?", "So who bent the lines?"
- Time-jump phrases: "Let's rewind." / "Rewind to 1945." / "It starts in 1802, in Madras."
- Twist markers: "Here's the twist." / "And the twist?" / "Then it gets stranger." / "Spoiler: it wasn't."
- Dry, understated humour; deadpan character one-liners (8 words max): "Me? I'm literally just a latitude.",
  "Non, merci.", "Special delivery! One farm!", "Am I late again?"
- Relatable asides: "And you thought your office meetings were long." / "You're welcome... I guess."
- End on a one-line insight that reframes the story: "They follow governments." / "Money was never the stone
  or the gold." / "The real ghosts down there are our spaceships." Then the sign-off.
- Scale anchors a global viewer knows (e.g. Eiffel Tower, London to Paris); India comparisons (Qutub Minar, Delhi to
  Mumbai, India's +5:30) are a welcome bonus, especially in the Hindi version.

## Words we use
"This is BHUGOL. Follow for more Geography Fun Facts." (fixed sign-off; Hindi: "ये है भूगोल। ऐसे ही मज़ेदार
जियोग्राफी फैक्ट्स के लिए फॉलो करें।") · "the story goes" · "about / around / nearly" for estimates ·
kilometres first.

## Words we never use (starter list: none appear in the 7 scripts; to be confirmed over time)
"guys", "hey everyone", "welcome back", "smash that like", "subscribe", "dive in", "let's get into it",
"insane", "mind-blowing", "crazy", "you won't believe", "game-changer", "secret".

## What we never claim
- No number without a source; estimates keep their hedge ("about 260", "over half").
- Legends and anecdotes are framed as "the story goes".
- No opinions on live disputes, Partition, communal or religious topics. Wars are told as geography and
  history, never with a side.
- No likeness claims about real historical people in art.

## Format
- 60-90 s vertical Short, English + Hindi on the same visuals, no intro, no channel greeting.
- Hook in the first 2 s, the question by about 5-8 s, payoff at 70-80%, a twist ending, sign-off and end card.
- Narrator: Gemini Sadaltager; characters: Gemini Puck, pitch-shifted per character.

## Style to evaluate later
"India's Hidden Superpower" (early video) uses a different style: an explanatory tone, a moral ending and a
different sign-off. No verdict yet; compare its performance with later videos before deciding.

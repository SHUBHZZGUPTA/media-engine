On 13 November 2026, AQI.in data showed 86 of the world's 100 most polluted cities in India, and Delhi's IIT station hit 488 on India's AQI scale, which tops out at 500. The Himalayas wall off the north, hills close the south, and in November cold air off the Tibetan Plateau sits under warmer air like a lid on a pot.
